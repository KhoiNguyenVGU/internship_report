\chapter{Conclusions}
\label{chap:conclusions}

This report has documented my four-month Business Analyst internship undertaken at AIVISION Joint Stock Company from March to July 2026. The internship spanned four main areas of work: wireframing and UI design, project management, requirements gathering and documentation, and quality control for AI-powered products.

Across these areas, a range of concrete deliverables were produced: 3 complete Figma wireframe sets for the GeneSolutions mobile application (approved by the client and scheduled for development), approximately 10 structured requirements and documentation artefacts across five concurrent client projects, 30+ sprint boards maintained weekly on Azure DevOps, and 2 monthly QC reports for the Masan AI product covering 1,000 annotated images each and shared with both the client and the development team.

The internship also provided substantial professional development. My technical proficiency was built across Figma, Azure DevOps, Label Studio, and Excel, alongside domain exposure to computer vision, agentic AI, FMCG workflows, and the full Software Development Life Cycle. Equally important were the soft skills developed in practice: client communication, meeting facilitation, requirements elicitation, cross-functional coordination, and managing multiple parallel workstreams under time pressure.

The experience revealed a meaningful gap between academic preparation and professional practice. Real projects are more ambiguous and more demanding in terms of interpersonal communication than coursework reflects. Learning to confirm rather than assume, to structure rather than improvise, and to adapt to shifting requirements was the most valuable dimension of the placement.

Overall, my internship at AIVISION provided a rigorous and well-rounded introduction to BA work in a technology company at the intersection of AI, software development, and enterprise client services. It has laid a practical foundation that will strengthen both my final year of study and the career path that follows.
