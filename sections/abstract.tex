\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

In this report, I documented a four-month (March 11 - July 10, 2026) practical training internship as a Business Analyst Intern at AIVISION Joint Stock Company, an AI and software focused company based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam that builds and outsources applications to major technology clients.

The core challenge that I had to face throughout the internship was bridging the communication gap between external customers and the internal development team. Operating in the role of a Business Analyst Intern, my assigned tasks revolve around the software development lifecycle, including requirements gathering, wireframe and UI designing, project management, documentation, and quality control.

As for the tools, I carried out my work using some of the most popular industry-standard tool kits, which include Azure DevOps for project tracking and collaboration, Figma and FigJam for wireframing and designing, and React with TypeScript for front-end development. Microsoft Office and AI tools were also utilized for daily Business Analysis tasks, e.g., NotebookLM helps me generate meeting minutes, while Claude/ChatGPT aids me in daily task management and documentation. Finally, as for quality control, Label Studio was used for testing the developed solutions.

As a result of this internship, I learned how to successfully translate customer requirements into clear, actionable insights for the development team. Furthermore, I also developed the essential toolkit in requirements gathering, project management, wireframe design, and quality control, skills in which are crucial for the development of a Business Analyst in a modern software organization.
