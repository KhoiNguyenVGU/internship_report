\chapter{Introduction}

Internship and practical training is a compulsory module of the Computer Science program at the Vietnamese-German University (VGU), weighing at 15 ECTS. It is designed and included in the curriculum to provide students with hands-on practice in professional working environments before entering the work industry. I wrote this report as proof and resources of my four-month internship journey at AIVISION Joint Stock Company from March 11 to July 10, 2026.

At the beginning in February, when I was browsing for internship opportunities on LinkedIn, I stumbled on a BA Intern position which AIVISION was offering and it immediately caught my attention. Even with no prior professional experience in the role, I was still drawn to the position for several practical reasons: first, it was a manageable four-month commitment, second, the office was close to my home, third, the internship came with a reasonable allowance, and finally, the company had a strong professional reputation. After submitting my CV, I was invited for an interview and was fortunately enough to pass and receive an offer shortly after.

My internship journey at AIVision began with an onboarding period where I mainly assisted a senior Business Analyst on smaller-scale outsourcing software projects for domestic clients such as GeneSolutions, a Vietnam-based company. Even though things started out slowly, this initial phase really helped me build foundational skills in requirements gathering, wireframing, and documentation in a supported environment. After about one month, I started to take on greater independence and responsibility by beginning to manage three to five larger AI-driven projects simultaneously for major enterprise clients, including Masan, Syngenta, P\&G, andINNOVA US.

By pursuing this opportunity, my main personal goal apart from fulfilling the internship requirements was straightforward: to learn what it takes to work as a Business Analyst in a real world scenario, not from textbooks, but through real interactions with clients and development teams, solving real problems and delivering real solutions.

For easier navigation, I have structured the report as follows. Chapter~\ref{chap:org} provides a profile of AIVision as an organization. Finally, Chapters~\ref{chap:lessons} and~\ref{chap:conclusions} offer my personal reflections and concluding remarks, respectively.
