\section{Company Overview and History}

AIVision Joint Stock Company (\url{aivision.vn}) is a company specialised in artificial intelligence and software development that is based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. It was founded in January 2023 by Dr.\ Giang Vo, a doctorate-level AI scientist with fifteen years of international research and industry experience in Singapore and the United States. With the major goal of bringing cutting-edge AI technology to the Vietnamese market, AIVision grew at remarkable speed, soon progressing into a million-dollar startup within its first few years of operation.

\section{Leadership and Workforce}

AIVision possesses a highly specialised technical workforce, comprising of over 100 AI experts. Elite professionals build the foundation for leadership and research teams, who are holding PhD degrees from prestigious international universities, such as the National University of Singapore (NUS) and the University of Cambridge. Some are also senior engineers with prior experience at major global technology firms such as NVIDIA (USA), alongside specialists from Germany and Singapore. This high quality and professional workforce acts as the key to thrust the company's ability to develop proprietary, state-of-the-art AI solutions at a commercially competitive pace.

\section{Core Products and Services}

AIVision develops proprietary AI models and software products, which retains complete intellectual property rights. The products are mainly optimised for the Southeast Asian market, but can also be deployed and operate at a global level. The four core AI solutions offered by AIVision are listed below:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{AI Display Tracking.} An automated Computer Vision system that evaluates retail product displays by recognising products, counting Stock Keeping Units (SKUs), and scoring Planogram compliance. A 360-degree panorama stitching and fraud detection feature is also incorporated, achieving 97–98\% accuracy with latency under 1 second.

    \item \textbf{Agentic AI.} A next-generation multi-agent platform integrating Large Language Models (LLMs) such as GPT and Llama. Regarding as a ``Digital Operations Copilot'', it aids in automating complex business workflows, customer service interactions, document summarisation, and data analysis via autonomous planning and operation.

    \item \textbf{AI Camera Analytics.} A smart video analysis system integrated into surveillance cameras in existing infrastructure, designed to automatically detect retail fraud, monitor factory security, perform Automatic Number Plate Recognition, and generate foot-traffic heatmaps.

    \item \textbf{Custom AI Software.} Customized enterprise software including a variety of usage, such as an AI HR Platform that automates the recruitment process via CV screening and intelligent candidate matching, as well as Computer Vision applications for healthcare to track and analyse dermatological treatments.
\end{itemize}

\section{Key Achievements and Client Portfolio}

Products and solutions of AIVision have been carefully tested and reviewed, making sure that they are robust and error-free in large-scale, stressful real-world production environments. As for the client portfolio, the company have had ample opportunities to work with major billion-dollar corporations and international brands within the past few years, including Masan, Pepsi, Marico, VNG, INNOVA US, etc. Most notably however, AIVision has successfully deployed its AI Display Scoring platform across Masan's network of over 500,000 retail points of sale by building a system capable of handling more than 5,000 concurrent users while also ensuring beyond expected customer satisfaction rate.

\section{Global Presence and Expansion Strategy}

Even though AIVision's main office is situated in Ho Chi Minh City, the company is still able to maintain a strong international presence with secondary offices in Singapore, Bangkok (Thailand), and Texas (USA). Even tody, the organization is still actively planning for a global expansion strategy with high ambition of becoming a strategic AI partner in large-scale markets, notably Australia, Mexico, and the broader United States in the upcoming years.
